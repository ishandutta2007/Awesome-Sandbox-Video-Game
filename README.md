# Awesome-Sandbox-Video-Game

# Top Sandbox Video Game Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Open-World Building, Crafting & Player-Driven Creation*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial sandbox games** and **open-source projects** that give players creative freedom — building, crafting, exploring, and modifying persistent worlds with minimal imposed goals.

**Examples** include Minecraft, Roblox, Terraria, Garry's Mod, Subnautica, No Man's Sky, Kerbal Space Program, Valheim, Scrap Mechanic, and Starbound (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Sandbox gaming has a strong open-source foundation. **Minetest** (now Luanti) leads as the most mature open-source voxel sandbox with 30,000+ GitHub stars, while **Veloren** delivers a stunning open-world RPG sandbox in Rust. **Terasology** pushes voxel boundaries with modular architecture, and **Cave Story** and **OpenTTD** demonstrate how open-source can sustain beloved sandbox experiences for decades. This section is heavily expanded.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/)**  
  **The best-selling video game of all time** with 300M+ copies sold. Infinite procedurally generated worlds, block-based building, survival mechanics, and **Redstone** for in-game computing. **The definitive sandbox game** — Java Edition supports extensive modding; Bedrock Edition offers cross-platform play.

- **[Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/)**  
  **The leading user-generated gaming platform** with 70M+ daily active users. Players create and publish games using **Roblox Studio** with Lua scripting. **The most accessible game creation platform** — millions of user-made experiences across every genre.

- **[Terraria](https://terraria.org/)**  
  **2D sandbox adventure** with 50M+ copies sold. Mining, crafting, building, and combat in procedurally generated worlds. **The definitive 2D sandbox** — massive content updates over a decade.

- **[Garry's Mod](https://gmod.playershops.com/)**  
  **Physics sandbox built on Source engine**. Players manipulate objects, build contraptions, and create custom game modes with Lua scripting. **The most moddable sandbox** — thousands of community addons.

- **[Subnautica](https://unknownworlds.com/subnautica/)**  
  **Underwater survival sandbox** with base building, resource gathering, and exploration. **The best underwater sandbox** — narrative-driven with creative mode.

- **[No Man's Sky](https://www.nomanssky.com/)**  
  **Procedurally generated universe sandbox** with 18 quintillion planets. Base building, exploration, and multiplayer. **The most ambitious procedural sandbox** — continuous free updates since 2016.

- **[Kerbal Space Program](https://www.kerbalspaceprogram.com/)**  
  **Physics-based space program sandbox** where players build rockets, planes, and space stations. **The definitive engineering sandbox** — realistic orbital mechanics with humorous Kerbals.

- **[Valheim](https://www.valheimgame.com/)**  
  **Viking survival sandbox** with building, crafting, and exploration. **The best survival-building sandbox** — procedurally generated Norse worlds.

- **[Scrap Mechanic](https://scrapmechanic.com/)**  
  **Physics-based construction sandbox** where players build vehicles, machines, and contraptions. **The best engineering sandbox for creative builders** .

- **[Starbound](https://playstarbound.com/)**  
  **2D space sandbox adventure** with procedurally generated planets, building, and crafting. **The best space-faring 2D sandbox** .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Luanti (formerly Minetest)](https://github.com/luanti-org/luanti)**  
  **The leading open-source voxel sandbox game engine**, LGPL-2.1 licensed with **30,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not a game — a game engine** with a small core and **extensive modding via Lua** . **Thousands of community mods and games** including MineClone (Minecraft-like), NodeCore, and Voxelgarden . **Runs on low-end hardware** — even Raspberry Pi . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, FreeBSD) . **The de facto open-source Minecraft alternative** — completely free with no microtransactions . **Best for players wanting infinite customization and creators building voxel games** .

- **[Veloren](https://github.com/veloren/veloren)**  
  **Open-source multiplayer voxel RPG written in Rust**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **8,000+ GitHub stars** . **Inspired by Cube World, Legend of Zelda, Dwarf Fortress, and Minecraft** . Features **procedurally generated world with varied biomes, dungeons, and NPCs** . **Combat, crafting, exploration, and building** in a persistent multiplayer world . **Written in Rust** for performance and safety . **The most promising open-source RPG sandbox** — active development with playable alpha . **Best for players wanting a cooperative RPG sandbox experience** .

- **[Terasology](https://github.com/MovingBlocks/Terasology)**  
  **Open-source voxel world game with modular architecture**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **3,500+ GitHub stars** . **Award-winning project** — started as a tech demo by Benjamin Porter . **Modular systems** for world generation, rendering, and gameplay . **Java-based with Gradle** build system . **The most architecturally ambitious voxel sandbox** — designed for extensibility and research . **Best for developers wanting a voxel engine foundation** .

- **[MineClone2](https://github.com/mineclone2/mineclone2)**  
  **The most complete Minecraft-like game for Luanti**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **1,500+ GitHub stars** . **Faithful recreation of Minecraft mechanics** — survival, crafting, mobs, nether, end, and redstone-like systems . **Actively maintained** with regular updates . **Best for players wanting Minecraft gameplay in a fully open-source package** .

- **[Mineclonia](https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/mineclonia)**  
  **Another Minecraft-like game for Luanti**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Focused on stability and performance** — alternative to MineClone2 . **Best for players wanting a lighter Minecraft experience** .

- **[OpenTTD](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD)**  
  **Open-source transport simulation sandbox**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Reimplementation of Transport Tycoon Deluxe** with massive improvements . **Build transport networks, manage finances, and compete with AI** . **The definitive open-source business sandbox** — active for 20+ years . **Best for players wanting economic sandbox gameplay** .

- **[OpenRCT2](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2)**  
  **Open-source reimplementation of RollerCoaster Tycoon 2**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Build and manage amusement parks** with improved multiplayer, larger maps, and mod support . **The best open-source theme park sandbox** . **Best for players wanting creative park-building** .

- **[0 A.D.](https://github.com/0ad/0ad)**  
  **Open-source real-time strategy game**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Historical warfare sandbox** — build cities, manage economies, and command armies . **The most ambitious open-source RTS** — 20+ years in development . **Best for players wanting historical strategy sandbox** .

- **[Widelands](https://github.com/widelands/widelands)**  
  **Open-source real-time strategy and building game**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Settle, build, and conquer** — inspired by Settlers II . **The best open-source Settlers-like sandbox** . **Best for players wanting settlement-building gameplay** .

- **[Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA)**  
  **Open-source post-apocalyptic survival sandbox**, CC-BY-SA licensed . **Deep crafting, building, and survival mechanics** — roguelike with endless depth . **The most complex open-source survival sandbox** . **Best for players wanting hardcore survival simulation** .

- **[Endless Sky](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky)**  
  **Open-source space trading and combat sandbox**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **4,000+ GitHub stars** . **Explore, trade, fight, and build a fleet** — inspired by Escape Velocity . **The best open-source space sandbox** . **Best for players wanting open-universe space trading** .

- **[Pioneer](https://github.com/pioneerspacesim/pioneer)**  
  **Open-source space exploration and combat sandbox**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Procedurally generated galaxy** — trade, explore, and fight across star systems . **The most ambitious open-source space sim sandbox** . **Best for players wanting hard sci-fi exploration** .

- **[Simutrans](https://github.com/simutrans/simutrans)**  
  **Open-source transport simulation game**, Artistic License 2.0 . **Build transport networks** — passengers, freight, and mail . **The best open-source transport sandbox after OpenTTD** . **Best for players wanting economic transport simulation** .

- **[Rigs of Rods](https://github.com/RigsOfRods/rigs-of-rods)**  
  **Open-source vehicle simulation sandbox**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Soft-body physics** for realistic vehicle deformation . **Build and drive vehicles** — trucks, cars, planes, and boats . **The best open-source physics sandbox** . **Best for players wanting vehicle engineering sandbox** .

- **[The Powder Toy](https://github.com/The-Powder-Toy/The-Powder-Toy)**  
  **Open-source physics sandbox game**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **3,000+ GitHub stars** . **Simulate particles, liquids, gases, and electronics** — falling-sand game with realistic physics . **The definitive open-source falling-sand sandbox** . **Best for players wanting particle physics experimentation** .

**Frameworks for building custom sandbox games**: Choose based on scope and platform. **Luanti** for voxel sandbox engines with Lua modding . **Veloren** for Rust-based multiplayer RPG sandboxes . **Terasology** for modular voxel architecture research . **OpenTTD** for economic/transport sandbox foundations . **The Powder Toy** for particle physics simulation . **OpenRCT2** for theme park management sandboxes . **Cataclysm: DDA** for deep survival simulation . Note that true commercial sandbox games with massive player bases, cross-platform infrastructure, and continuous live-service updates remain primarily proprietary; open-source projects provide strong engine, modding, and gameplay foundations that require community building and hosting infrastructure for complete sandbox experiences.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Sandbox games often support user-generated content and mods. **Verify mod sources** — malicious mods can compromise systems. Use official repositories and community-vetted sources.
- **Multiplayer sandbox servers require administration** — griefing, harassment, and abuse are common. Plan for moderation tools and community guidelines.
- **Open-source sandbox games vary in polish** — Luanti's default game is minimal; MineClone2 provides Minecraft-like completeness but may lag behind upstream Minecraft features . Veloren is playable alpha with ongoing development .
- **Some open-source games require original game assets** — OpenMW, DevilutionX, and OpenRA require files from the original commercial games. They are engine reimplementations, not full game distributions .
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong engine, modding, and gameplay foundations, but **massive multiplayer infrastructure, live-service operations, and cross-platform matchmaking** remain primarily commercial offerings.
